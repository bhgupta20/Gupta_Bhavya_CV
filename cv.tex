\section{\textbf{Research Highlights}}
\vspace{1mm}
\small{
I am a physics graduate student at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, interested in developing software for rapid transient detection and low-latency alert generation in time-domain astronomy. My research background includes machine-learning methods for gravitational-wave detection, with an emphasis on accelerating searches and enabling multi-messenger astronomy. I am particularly interested in building scalable, real-time computational systems that identify astrophysical transients and facilitate timely follow-up observations across multiple messengers to answer fundamental questions about our universe.
}
